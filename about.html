About Halfman Engineering

Four Decades of Engineering Experience

Halfman Engineering has spent more than 40 years building engineering and manufacturing capability for the automotive industry — including over 30 years as a trusted vendor to Pak Suzuki.

Our Story

Engineering Experience, Proven Over Time

Halfman Engineering's history is built on practical, hands-on engineering — machining, tooling and fabricating components for the demands of automotive manufacturing.

Over more than 40 years, that experience has been shaped by direct work within the automotive supply chain, including a vendor relationship with Pak Suzuki that has continued for over 30 years. Alongside this, Halfman Engineering has engaged with a range of other automotive manufacturers, building broader industry knowledge in CNC machining, die design and manufacturing, spot welding and checking fixture development.

That combination of longevity and hands-on manufacturing work has shaped a company built around engineering discipline, production reliability and a continued commitment to developing its capabilities further — including a focus on expanding into international export markets.

Our Mission

Precision, Reliability and Long-Term Partnership

To deliver precision manufacturing and engineering solutions that automotive partners can depend on — grounded in reliability, engineering excellence and customer satisfaction, and built to support long-term partnerships as Halfman Engineering grows into international markets.
