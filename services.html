Engineering & Manufacturing Capabilities

Integrated Manufacturing Capabilities

Engineering, tooling, production, machining, welding, and inspection all under one roof.

Our Core Capabilities

OUR CORE CAPABILITIES

01

Press Shop

Our Press Shop is a key strength of our production department, equipped with a comprehensive range of presses of various capacities and types, including England-made and locally manufactured equipment. The Press Shop is supported by an integrated shearing section and is well-equipped to handle high-volume sheet metal blanking, stamping, drawing, forming, piercing, trimming, and press manufacturing for automotive components. Our capabilities include progressive, transfer, and line-die operations, supported by precise tonnage control to ensure consistent and accurate production.

Learn More
02

Die & Tooling

Halfman Engineering operates a state-of-the-art Tool & Die facility with the capability to design and manufacture production dies and checking fixtures directly from CAD data. Our toolroom supports the development of progressive, transfer, and line dies engineered for precision, consistency, and high-volume automotive production. We also carry out regular inspection, preventive maintenance, and refurbishment of existing dies to ensure reliable performance, extended tool life, and consistent component quality throughout production.

Learn More
03

CNC Machining

Precision CNC machining of components, tooling, dies, fixtures, and production parts with a strong focus on dimensional accuracy, consistency, and quality. Our CNC machining capabilities enable us to manufacture precision-engineered components with complex profiles, detailed features, and tight dimensional requirements. From automotive components and production parts to dies, tooling, jigs, and fixtures, we provide reliable machining solutions tailored to specific design and production requirements, ensuring accuracy, repeatability, and consistent performance.

Learn More
04

CAD/CAM Designing

Advanced CAD/CAM design, engineering development, and CNC programming for precision components, dies, tooling, jigs, fixtures, and production applications. Our design capabilities cover AutoCAD, Creo, AutoForm, and CATIA, enabling us to develop accurate 2D drawings, detailed 3D models, tooling designs, and production-ready engineering data. From initial concept and design development to CAM programming and validated NC code, we provide a complete digital engineering workflow focused on precision, manufacturability, and production efficiency.

Learn More
05

Welding Shop

Our Welding Shop is a vital part of our production facility, dedicated to the precise assembly of components, sub-components, and accessories for automotive applications. Equipped with modern welding facilities and experienced technical personnel, we deliver consistent, reliable, and high-quality welded assemblies. Our capabilities include GMAW/MIG Welding (200–300 Amp), PLC-controlled fixed spot welding (35 kVA), portable spot welding (30 kVA), SMAW, and buffing & polishing. We support controlled welding and fabrication for structural and non-structural automotive assemblies, maintaining precision, consistency, and dependable weld quality throughout production.

Learn More
06

Quality Department

Comprehensive quality control and dimensional inspection to ensure components consistently meet specified tolerances, design requirements, and applicable manufacturing standards. Our quality assurance processes include dimensional verification, precision measurement, gauge calibration, and inspection of critical features throughout the production cycle. We maintain accurate inspection records and support complete PPAP documentation, ensuring traceability, consistency, and compliance with customer-specific quality requirements.
