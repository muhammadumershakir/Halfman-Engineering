CORE ENGINEERING SERVICES

01

Designing (AUTO CAD, CREO, AUTO FORM & CATIA)

Advanced CAD/CAM design, engineering development, and CNC programming for precision components, dies, tooling, jigs, fixtures, and production applications. Our design capabilities cover AutoCAD, Creo, AutoForm, and CATIA, enabling us to develop accurate 2D drawings, detailed 3D models, tooling designs, and production-ready engineering data. From initial concept and design development to CAM programming and validated NC code, we provide a complete digital engineering workflow focused on precision, manufacturability, and production efficiency.

02

Welding jig Development

Our Welding jig Development services provide precision-engineered fixtures for accurate positioning, alignment, and secure holding of components during welding operations. We develop robust and reliable fixtures according to specific component designs and production requirements, ensuring consistent positioning, dimensional accuracy, repeatability, and weld quality. Our welding fixtures are designed to support efficient production while minimizing component movement and maintaining accurate assembly throughout the welding process.

03

Bumpers jig development

Our Bumper jig Development services provide precision-engineered jigs and fixtures for accurate positioning, alignment, and assembly of automotive bumper components. We develop robust and reliable jig solutions based on specific component designs and production requirements, ensuring proper fitment, dimensional accuracy, repeatability, and consistent assembly quality. Our bumper jigs are designed to support efficient production while maintaining precise component positioning throughout the manufacturing and assembly process.

Trollies and Bins Fabrication
