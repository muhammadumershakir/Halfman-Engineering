Technical RFQ Guidelines

What to Include for Fast Engineering & Export Quotations

Whether you are an automotive OEM in Pakistan or an international procurement team in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, Europe, North America, or Australia, sharing complete technical parameters helps our engineering desk turn around accurate DFM and cost breakdowns quickly.

1. 2D & 3D CAD Files

Email or WhatsApp your 3D models (STEP, IGES, Parasolid) and 2D drawings (DXF, PDF) with critical GD&T tolerances for CNC machining or die tooling.

2. Material Grade & Thickness

Specify sheet thickness (0.5 mm–6.0 mm) and standard (JIS G3141 SPCC/SPHC, SGCC galvanized, high-tensile steel, aluminum, or ASTM/DIN equivalents) for 30T–500T press stamping.

3. Volume & Secondary Operations

Include prototype vs. annual production batch quantities, plus required PLC spot/MIG welding, Checking Fixtures (CF), or surface treatment.

4. Quality & Inspection Deliverables

Indicate required ISO 9001:2015 quality documentation, CMM dimensional inspection reports, First-Article Inspection (FAI), or OEM PPAP submission packages.
