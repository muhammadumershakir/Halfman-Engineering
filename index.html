Phase 01 // Engineering

Part Specification, 3D CAD & DFM Review

We analyze OEM 2D/3D engineering drawings, material grade specifications (SPCC, SPHC, high-tensile steel), and forming feasibility to lock in zero-defect manufacturing parameters.

  • Sheet metal grain & springback analysis
  • Strip layout & blank optimization
Phase 02 // Tooling

In-House Die Engineering & CNC Machining

Our toolroom machines progressive, compound, and forming dies on high-precision CNC centers, paired with custom Checking Fixtures (CF) and welding jigs.

  • ±0.01mm CNC toolpath accuracy
  • Hardened tool steel die assemblies
Phase 03 // Stamping

High-Tonnage Press Shop Operations

Validated dies enter our mechanical and hydraulic press bays for automated blanking, deep drawing, piercing, and multi-stage forming at scale.

  • Consistent press tonnage & stroke control
  • First-off piece dimensional verification
Phase 04 // Assembly

Resistance Spot Welding & Sub-Assembly

Stamped sub-components are locked into dedicated pneumatic jigs for multi-point resistance spot welding, projection nut welding, and structural joining.

  • Weld nugget peel & strength testing
  • Datum-locked fixture repeatability
Phase 05 // Quality Assurance

Checking Fixture Validation & OEM Dispatch

Every production lot undergoes rigorous inspection on dedicated Checking Fixtures (CF) and digital gauges under ISO 9001:2015 protocols before OEM delivery.

  • 100% Go/No-Go fixture verification
  • Full batch traceability & certification
